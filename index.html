About the Academy

Practical design education,
without the fluff

Online Learnify Academy was founded to close the gap between design theory and real client work. Instead of long lectures, every course is built around briefs, feedback and portfolio pieces — so students leave with skills they can use the same week.

Since our first batch, we've trained more than a thousand students across Canva, Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator, with a curriculum that keeps pace with what studios and freelance clients actually ask for.

Meet the Founder

Muhammad Hamza Awais

Founder & CEO, Online Learnify Academy

Muhammad Hamza Awais is the Founder & CEO of Online Learnify, established with a vision to empower students through practical graphic design education and help them build successful creative careers. Through hands-on learning, real-world projects, and industry-focused training, he has trained 1K+ students in tools including Canva, Adobe Illustrator, and Adobe Photoshop.

Beyond design training, his focus is helping students move from learning to earning. Through mentorship,career guidance, internships, and work opportunities, he has helped 50+ students take their first steps toward professional careers in graphic design, freelancing, and creative work.
